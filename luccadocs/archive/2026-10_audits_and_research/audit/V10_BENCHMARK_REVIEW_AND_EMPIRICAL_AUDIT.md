## 4. Analysis of Reasoning Failure Modes

### Overthinking Failure in Test-Time Compute (Extended Thinking)
Extended Thinking exhibited a systemic degradation on exact mathematical domains:
1. **CAS & Number Theory Degradation (40.0% accuracy):**
   - In tasks `SEALED_SYMPY_01` ($k=150$-th prime calculation), `SEALED_SYMPY_04` (sum of divisors), and `SEALED_SYMPY_05` (modular arithmetic), Extended Thinking generated between 1,800 and 2,048 tokens of self-deliberation.
   - During the internal dialogue, the model repeatedly proposed intermediate arithmetic conjectures, drifted from the original question constraints, and either ran out of tokens or concluded with a hallucinated integer (e.g. predicting `127` instead of `863`, `1` instead of `148`, and `13` instead of `585`).
2. **Formal Translation Degradation (40.0% accuracy):**
   - On tasks requiring evaluating algorithmically specified loops (`SEALED_TRANS_02`, `03`, `05`), Extended Thinking attempted manual step-by-step mental simulation, losing track of loop counter state at iterations $i > 5$.
   - Compound synthesis, by contrast, emitted a 6-line Python script that executed in $3.5$ ms inside the Docker sandbox, producing the ground-truth value deterministically.

### Turn 1 Latency Advantage of Compound Execution
- **Compound Mean Latency:** $\approx 5.8$ seconds (median $\approx 4.5$ seconds).
- **Extended Thinking Mean Latency:** $\approx 31.4$ seconds (frequently hitting 40.7s when saturating 2,048 tokens).
- **Efficiency Factor:** Compound Neurosymbolic execution delivered a **5.4× speedup** over Extended Thinking while eliminating the 40% error rate.

## 5. Audit of Diagnostic Suite Fixes

### 1. GPQA Diamond Token-Cap Fix
- **Diagnostic Finding:** Auditing the historical `gpqa_diamond_1791835888.json` report revealed that **20 of 30 Direct predictions returned `direct_prediction: null`**. The 10.0% reported accuracy was not a reasoning ceiling, but an artifact of `max_tokens=256` truncating multi-paragraph scientific responses before the model reached "(A), (B), (C), or (D)".
- **Fix Implemented:** Increased `max_tokens` from 256 to 2048 in [`scripts/run_gpqa_diamond_benchmark.py`](file:///C:/Projects/ProjectLucca/scripts/run_gpqa_diamond_benchmark.py).
- **Live Empirical Verification:** Smoke-testing verified that the model now generates full scientific explanations and reliably outputs explicit choice tokens `(A)`, `(B)`, `(C)`, or `(D)` with zero `null` returns.

### 2. BFCL v3 Grader Comma-Splitting Fix
- **Diagnostic Finding:** In v1.37 evaluations, function calls with tuple/list arguments (e.g. `coords=(10, 20)`) were corrupted by raw string splitting on commas.
- **Fix Implemented:** In [`scripts/run_bfcl_benchmark.py`](file:///C:/Projects/ProjectLucca/scripts/run_bfcl_benchmark.py), the evaluation pipeline now uses Python AST parsing (`ast.parse`) with recursive semantic type matching (`compare_arg_values`), treating `[10, 20]` and `(10, 20)` as equivalent sequence arguments.

## 6. Recommendations & Improvement Horizon (v1.39)

1. **Higher-Complexity Parametric Holdouts:**
   - The current $N=125$ parametric generator demonstrates that 12B Direct greedy decoding can often solve simple, single-clause arithmetic and epistemic stories when answers are small integers.
   - For v1.39, expand the parametric generator with:
     - 4-agent, 3-location epistemic chains involving second-order false beliefs (*"What does Alice believe that Bob thinks is in the drawer?"*).
     - Diophantine systems with large solution spaces ($> 10^5$) requiring bounded integer programming.
2. **Pre-Emptive Docker Worker Keepalive:**
   - On the very first invocation in a benchmark run, spawning the persistent WSL2 Docker worker takes $\approx 3.5$ seconds. Subsequent invocations execute in $< 2$ ms. A warm-up ping in `main()` will normalize Turn 1 benchmark latency across all suites.
3. **Dual-Formulation Consensus for Unchecked Tasks:**
   - For open-ended natural language reasoning without clear ground-truth harnesses, expand `synthesize_checked_formalization` to automatically compare SymPy algebraic vs. algorithmic simulation formulations, alerting the user when the two formalizations disagree.
